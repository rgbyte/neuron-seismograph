    Neuron Seismograph // Workflow & Tooling Guide

    STEP 01 · SCAN PROMPT

    Map Any Prompt Into 3D

    Type any prompt into the bottom-right dock and click Scan (or paste a full transcript in Layer Scanner). The server queries /api/infer/view across all 32 layers and lights up the top 120 firing neurons in 3D.

    STEP 02 · INSPECT & PINPOINT

    Explore 3D Orbs & Logit Lens

    Click any floating 3D pin, any row in Spiking Neurons, or any word in the bottom Token Seismograph. The camera flies to that layer, renders its 1-hop synaptic arcs, and shows the vocabulary words it promotes (+) or suppresses (−).

    STEP 03 · CLAMP ACTIVATION

    Tune the Steering Dose

    Drag the Dose Clamp slider (−3.0× to +3.0×) or click a preset (−0.8×, 0×, +0.8×). Watch the 3D gyroscope orb and laminar particle stream respond in real time.

    STEP 04 · STREAM & VERIFY

    Run 3-Way Live Rollout

    Click ▶ Stream to stream three simultaneous generations (Boosted +Dose, Baseline 0.0×, Suppressed −Dose) and compare distinct-4 coherence. Click ■ Stop anytime to halt the GPU forward pass.

    Need a clean slate? Click ↺ Reset in the top bar at any time to restore the canonical 3-Neuron Refusal Dam circuit, reset the 3D camera orbit, and set the dose back to −0.8×.
    32 Fibonacci Irises

    Each disc represents one MLP layer of Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B (L0 on the left to L31 on the right). All 9,216 gate neurons in a layer are arranged in a Golden-Angle spiral (θ = n × 137.5°) so every neuron has a deterministic 3D coordinate.

    Supernovas & Orbs

    When a prompt is scanned, up to 120 neurons with the highest activation z-scores ignite as bright stars (Emerald/Gold for positive +σ excitation, Crimson for negative −σ dips). The top 4 spiking neurons receive rotating dual-ring gyroscope orbs and clickable 3D pins.

    1-Hop Synaptic Arcs

    Curved Quadratic Bezier filaments connect your currently focused neuron to its strongest direct 1-hop upstream/downstream synaptic partners (|hop| = 1 from /api/web), plus the relay spine linking the top 4 prompt orbs in layer order.

    3D Seismic Horizon

    Switch to Horizon in the top bar to view all 32 layers as a 3D ridgeline waterfall where X is token position, Z is layer depth (L0–L31), and Y is activation z-score amplitude.
